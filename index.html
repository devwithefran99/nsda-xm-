What Drives Me

Career Mission / Vision

My Mission

To provide evidence-based, compassionate cardiovascular care that prioritizes patient outcomes, dignity, and long-term well-being — making quality cardiac medicine accessible to all.

My Vision

A Bangladesh where preventable heart disease is rare, patients are empowered through education, and every individual has access to high-quality cardiac care close to home.

Core Values

Integrity in every diagnosis. Empathy in every consultation. Excellence in every procedure. These values are the foundation of everything I do inside and outside the clinic.

What I Offer

Medical Services

Comprehensive cardiac and internal medicine services tailored to your needs

Cardiac Consultation

Thorough evaluation of heart health, risk stratification, and personalised treatment planning for all cardiac conditions.

ECG & Echo

Advanced electrocardiography and echocardiography to assess heart structure, rhythm, and function with precision.

Hypertension Management

Lifestyle counselling, medication management, and regular monitoring programs to keep blood pressure under control.

Diabetes & Heart Care

Integrated management of diabetes-related cardiovascular risk, tailored for long-term metabolic and heart health.

Preventive Cardiology

Comprehensive health screenings and preventive strategies to reduce the risk of heart attack and stroke before symptoms develop.

Teleconsultation

Convenient online video consultations for follow-ups, prescription renewals, and second opinions from the comfort of home.
